Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 6, 2026

McDaid & Sons Construction LLC ("McDaid & Sons Construction," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to information collected through our website, our online estimate request form, our Facebook and Instagram pages and lead forms, and any other interactions you have with us (collectively, the "Services").

By using our Services or submitting information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use our Services or submit your information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you request a free estimate or otherwise contact us — through our website form, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, phone, email, or text — we may collect:

  • Your first and last name;
  • Your phone number and email address;
  • Your property address and city;
  • The service you are interested in (roofing, windows, siding, or a combination);
  • How you heard about us and, if applicable, who referred you; and
  • Any additional details you choose to share about your project, timeline, or property.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. We collect this information using cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies (see "Cookies and Tracking Technologies" below).

Information from Third Parties

If you contact us through a third-party platform — for example, by submitting a lead form on Facebook or Instagram (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) — we receive the information you provide on that platform, subject to that platform's own privacy policy and your settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and contact you to schedule and provide a free estimate;
  • Provide, perform, and follow up on the roofing, window, siding, and related services you request;
  • Communicate with you by phone, email, and text message about your request, appointment, project, or account;
  • Coordinate work with our employees, subcontractors, and suppliers;
  • Operate, maintain, improve, and secure our website and Services;
  • Understand how our marketing performs and reach homeowners who may be interested in our services; and
  • Comply with our legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes. We share information only as described below:

  • Subcontractors, carpenters, and suppliers. We share the information necessary to assess, schedule, and complete your project with the subcontractors, carpenters, and material suppliers we engage to perform the work.
  • Service providers. We use trusted third-party providers to operate our business — for example, form and data hosting (Google), website hosting, communications tools, and analytics and advertising platforms. These providers may process your information only on our behalf and in accordance with our instructions.
  • Advertising platforms. If you reach us through an ad or lead form, platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google may receive information to deliver and measure advertising, as described below.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our agreements; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of McDaid & Sons Construction, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Text Messaging (SMS)

By providing your phone number and submitting a request, you consent to receive calls and text messages from McDaid & Sons Construction at that number — including messages sent using automated technology — regarding your estimate, appointment, and project. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, and you can reply HELP for assistance. We will not share your mobile phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and measure and improve our advertising. This may include analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and advertising pixels (such as the Meta Pixel), which help us understand the effectiveness of our ads and reach homeowners who may be interested in our services.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of certain interest-based advertising through tools offered by the platforms and industry groups, including the Meta ad preferences settings, Google Ads Settings, and the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page at optout.aboutads.info. Disabling cookies may affect how the website functions.

6. Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

When you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, that information is collected through Meta's platform and provided to us so we can contact you about your request. Meta's handling of your information is governed by Meta's own Privacy Policy. We use the information we receive only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services, respond to your inquiry, complete your project, maintain business and accounting records, and comply with our legal obligations. When we no longer need your information, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect the personal information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, or to ask a question about this Privacy Policy. You may also opt out of marketing communications by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails or replying STOP to our texts. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law; we will honor those rights as required.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website and communications may contain links to third-party websites or platforms that we do not control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of our Services after any changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
