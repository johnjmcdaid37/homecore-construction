Why HomeCore Construction

Exteriors That Keep the Weather Out

Roofing, siding and windows are what protect everything else in your house. HomeCore Construction strips to the deck or the sheathing, fixes what is underneath, and installs underlayment, wrap and flashing properly before the finish goes on.

We do this work on its own and as part of renovations, additions and new homes, so we know how a roof or wall should be built from the framing out.

We work across Suffolk County and guide homeowners through permits and inspections. Suffolk County Home Improvement License HI-74242.

Questions

Roofing, Siding & Windows FAQ

Do you replace whole roofs?

Yes. We strip roofs to the deck, install synthetic underlayment and new architectural shingles, as shown on this page.

What siding do you install?

Vinyl and fiber cement siding, along with house wrap, trim and flashing. We match what suits your house and budget.

Do you replace windows?

Yes, as part of siding jobs, renovations and new construction. We frame, flash and install them so they stay dry.

Can you do the roof as part of a bigger renovation?

Yes. One of our Whole Home stories includes a full roof with new underlayment and shingles during the interior renovation.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

HomeCore Construction guides homeowners through permits and inspections, from filing to final sign off. Every Long Island town and village has its own building department and rules, so we check what your project needs before we price it, help with the paperwork, and meet the inspectors on site.

What areas do you serve?

HomeCore Construction handles exterior work across Suffolk County, from Bay Shore and Sayville to Huntington, Melville and the East End.
