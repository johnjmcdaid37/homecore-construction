What We Do

Whole-Home Remodeling & Construction

Interior or exterior, a single room or an entire home; one trusted team handles your project from first sketch to final walkthrough.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Full kitchen and bath remodels: cabinetry, counters, tile, plumbing, and lighting. We modernize the rooms you use most with craftsmanship that lasts and finishes you'll love.

Interior Renovations

Bedrooms, living spaces, basements, and full interior makeovers. From flooring and trim to walls and layouts, we transform how your home looks, feels, and functions.

Roofing, Siding & Windows

The exterior essentials that protect and beautify your home. Full roof replacements, new siding from vinyl to fiber cement, and energy-efficient windows, installed clean and built to last.

Additions & Structural

Room additions, dormers, and load-bearing changes. We handle both structural and non-structural work, opening up your floor plan or adding the square footage your family needs.

Custom Homes

Building from the ground up. We partner with you from plans through finishes to deliver a one-of-a-kind custom home built to your vision, your budget, and the highest standard of quality.

Spec & New Construction

Spec homes and new residential builds for homeowners and investors. Sound construction, smart layouts, and finishes that sell, delivered on schedule and on budget.

Our Story

HomeCore Construction

HomeCore Construction is a full-service home remodeling and construction company serving Long Island homeowners. From interior renovations like kitchens, baths, and basements to exterior work like roofing, siding, and windows, plus additions, structural work, and custom and spec home building, we take on projects of every size and see them through start to finish.


Our business is built entirely through word of mouth referrals, which tells you everything about how we operate. We believe that doing exceptional work and treating every customer with respect is the only marketing strategy that matters.


Whether you're updating a single room, reimagining your whole home, or building a new one from the ground up, we'd love to come take a look. Estimates are always free and always honest.
