An older home rebuilt and expanded: the house as we found it, demo, excavation, new steel, underpinning the basement walls in numbered sections, a new foundation with GMX Ultra-Shield waterproofing, then framing, sheathing, a shingled roof, a utility trench at the street, Tyvek house wrap, and new windows and doors going in.
