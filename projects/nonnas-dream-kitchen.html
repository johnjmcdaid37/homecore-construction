The Story

Built for the Cook of the Family

This kitchen belongs to an Italian grandmother who is happiest at home, cooking for the people she loves. Her old kitchen had dated cabinets, worn laminate counters, and a layout that made every meal harder work than it needed to be. She didn't want a showpiece. She wanted a kitchen she could really live in.

We took it down to the studs and rebuilt it around her: custom cabinetry in white shaker and white oak, quartz countertops, a farmhouse sink, and a large center island for prep and for family to gather around, with all-new stainless appliances, recessed lighting, and new flooring throughout.

Every decision came back to one question: does this make her time in the kitchen better? That's what shaped the details below.

The Details

Little Things That Change Everything

Custom touches designed around how she actually cooks, not what a catalog says a kitchen should have.

Built-In Wine Rack

A custom white oak wine bottle holder built right into the cabinetry, keeping her favorite bottles close at hand. A detail you won't find in an off-the-shelf kitchen.

Corner Carousel

Instead of a standard lazy Susan, the corner cabinet holds a carousel where the doors swing in with the shelves. The whole corner opens up to her: no reaching, no digging, no pots lost in the back.

Pull-Down Shelving

Upper cabinet shelving that pulls out and lowers down to meet her. She's a smaller cook, and now every shelf is within reach without a step stool. It's a level up in function you notice every single day.
