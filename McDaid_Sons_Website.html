What We Do

Roofing, Windows & Siding

Windows

Our Story

McDaid & Sons Construction

McDaid & Sons Construction was founded by JJ and John McDaid — a father and son team with over 30 years of combined construction experience on Long Island. We specialize in the three most impactful improvements you can make to your home's exterior: roofing, windows, and siding.


Our business was built entirely through word of mouth referrals — which tells you everything about how we operate. We believe that doing exceptional work and treating every customer with respect is the only marketing strategy that matters.


Whether you need a full roof replacement, new energy-efficient windows, or fresh siding that transforms your curb appeal — we'd love to come take a look. Estimates are always free and always honest.
